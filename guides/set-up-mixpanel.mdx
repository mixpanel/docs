How fast you get started with Mixpanel is mostly up to you. Smaller teams can usually move quickly, while larger teams may take longer because more people have to weigh in. What you control most is how long work sits waiting for a decision or for someone to pick it up.

## Three phases

These phases aren't strictly in order. You can work on the first two at the same time, and it's best to bring in your wider team once your data is flowing.

## Where a little effort now saves rework later

- **Start small.** Pick one use case and a short list of events. A focused plan is faster to build and easier to fix.
- **Set up identity first.** Without it, one person's activity can split across several profiles, and it's hard to fix once data is flowing.
- **Name at least two organization owners.** Owners have full control of your organization, so a second one means you're not depending on one person.
- **Tell Mixpanel's AI about your business.** A few facts about your company help it give more accurate answers.
