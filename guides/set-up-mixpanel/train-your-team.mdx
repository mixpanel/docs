## Overview

This phase gets your teammates using Mixpanel. You'll help them learn the basics, find answers on their own, and build a first report. Teammates need access first. [Choose how people get access](/guides/set-up-mixpanel/configure-your-account#choose-how-people-get-access)

<Info>
  Most people won't read a long guide before they try something. Let teammates build a report that answers a question they care about early, then point them to learning resources.
</Info>

**Done when:**

- Teammates know the basics
- They can find answers on their own
- They create their first report

## Get teammates up to speed

Help teammates go from having access to using Mixpanel on their own.

### Put reports on a board

### Put reports on a board

A board gives teammates a place to start, so no one has to open Mixpanel to a blank page. Save a few reports that answer the questions you wrote down in Phase 1 to a board, then share it with your teammates. [Boards](/docs/boards) · [Sharing and Permission](/docs/boards/sharing-and-permission)

### Help teammates build a report

Once teammates have somewhere to start, a report built from their own question shows each person what Mixpanel can do for their work. Ask each teammate to pick a question they care about and build a report that answers it. [Reports](/docs/reports)

### Help teammates find answers

Wait until teammates are comfortable with the interface and can build a basic report, then show them how to ask the Mixpanel Agent questions about your data. Knowing how to build a report is how they spot-check an answer, and an AI answer is worth confirming before anyone acts on it. [Mixpanel Agent](/docs/mixpanel-agent) · [Set up Mixpanel AI for accurate answers](/guides/use-mixpanel-ai/trust-ai-answers)

### Help teammates learn the basics

Most people won't start with learning content, and that's fine. Once teammates have built a report and tried asking questions, they know what they want to learn, so this is the right time to point them to more. Share an overview they can read, and Mixpanel University for anyone who wants to go deeper. [Mixpanel Introduction](/guides/mixpanel-introduction) · [Mixpanel Fundamentals](https://university.mixpanel.com/student/path/3411734-mixpanel-fundamentals)
