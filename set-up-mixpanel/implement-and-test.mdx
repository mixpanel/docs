Testing in development first means mistakes don't touch your real data. Your events and identity need to be right before real users generate data.

**Done when:**

- Events and users are set up correctly
- Your use cases are confirmed
- Data arrives in your development project

<Tip>
  Using an AI coding agent? Follow [Install with AI](/docs/quickstart/install-with-ai) instead. It installs and tests in your development environment.
</Tip>

## Set up yourself

### Create development and production projects

Keep test data out of your real data. Make one project for each. [Developer Environments](/docs/tracking-best-practices/developer-environments) · [Managing Projects](/docs/orgs-and-projects/managing-projects)

### Give your team access

Add your engineer and teammates with the right roles. Using single sign-on? Set it up first. [Roles & Permissions](/docs/orgs-and-projects/roles-and-permissions) · [Single Sign-On](/docs/access-security/single-sign-on)

### Choose how data gets in

Pick the method that fits your product, such as an SDK, a data pipeline, or a tool you already use. [Choosing the Right Method](/docs/tracking-methods/choosing-the-right-method) · [Tracking Methods](/docs/tracking-methods)

### Send your first events

Install the SDK and send the events from your plan to your development project. [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart) · [SDKs](/docs/tracking-methods/sdks) · [Tracking Best Practices](/docs/tracking-best-practices)

Tracking companies as groups? [Group Analytics Implementation Guide](/docs/data-structure/group-analytics/group-analytics-implementation)

{/* Optional: remove the comment markers to show this line.
  Building a web app? You can also turn on [Autocapture](/docs/tracking-methods/autocapture) for page views, clicks, and form inputs. It works alongside the events in your plan, and it needs a privacy check first.
  */}

### Identify your users

Set up identify when you first send data, so a person stays one person before and after login. [Identifying Users (Simplified)](/docs/tracking-methods/id-management/identifying-users-simplified) · [Identity Management Overview](/docs/tracking-methods/id-management)

### Check your events and identity

Watch your events arrive, then confirm each person shows up once, with the right properties. [Debugging](/docs/tracking-best-practices/debugging) · [Ensuring Data Quality](/guides/guides-by-workflow/ensure-data-quality)

No events showing? Start with the [Sending Data FAQ](/troubleshooting/sending-data).

### Clean up your event list

Add descriptions in Lexicon. Merge, hide, or drop events you don't need. [Lexicon](/docs/data-governance/lexicon) · [Data Clean-Up](/docs/data-governance/data-clean-up) · [Events](/docs/data-structure/events-and-properties)

### Keep personal data out

Send only what you need, and flag anything personal in your plan. [Data Privacy & PII Best Practices](/guides/guides-by-workflow/data-privacy)

On an Enterprise plan? Set rules for how new events get named and approved. [Data Standards](/docs/data-governance/data-standards) · [Event Approval](/docs/data-governance/event-approval)

### Confirm your use cases

Build a report from your development data that answers one of your top questions. If it can't, fix the tracking before you go live. [Board Templates](/docs/boards/templates) · [Insights](/docs/reports/insights)

## Next step

When your events and identity check out in development, go to [3. Launch to production](/guides/get-started/launch-to-production).
