This is when real users' data starts arriving. Comparing it with what you tested confirms the setup held up outside development.

**Done when:**

- Events reach your production project
- They match what you tested

<Note>
  You do this step whether you set up with AI or yourself. Used [Install with AI](/docs/quickstart/install-with-ai)? Review the code your agent wrote before you deploy it.
</Note>

## Same step, with or without AI

These steps are the same whether you set up with AI or yourself.

### Check you're ready to launch

Make sure your events and identity check out in development first. [Check your events and identity](/guides/get-started/implement-and-test#check-your-events-and-identity)

### Switch to your production project

Point your product at your production project, and stop sending test data to your development project. [Find your project token](/docs/orgs-and-projects/managing-projects#locate-current-project-token)

### Deploy your tracking

Review the code, then deploy it through your usual release process.

### Compare what arrives to what you tested

Build the same report in your production project and compare it with what you saw in development. [Confirm your use cases](/guides/get-started/implement-and-test#confirm-your-use-cases)

No events showing? [Missing or incomplete events](/docs/tracking-best-practices/debugging#missing-or-incomplete-events)

### Watch for problems

Set up alerts so you hear about drops or spikes before your team does. [Alerts](/docs/features/alerts)

On an Enterprise plan? [Data Volume Monitoring](/docs/data-governance/data-volume-monitoring) watches event volume for you.
