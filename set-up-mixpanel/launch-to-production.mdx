This is when real users' data starts arriving. Comparing it with what you tested confirms the setup held up outside development.

**Done when:**

- Events reach your production project
- They match what you tested

<Note>
  Used [Install with AI](/docs/quickstart/install-with-ai)? Review the code your agent wrote before you deploy it.
</Note>

## Either way

These steps are the same whether you set up with AI or yourself.

### Check you're ready to launch

Run through your tracking one more time before you switch. [Ensuring Data Quality](/guides/guides-by-workflow/ensure-data-quality)

### Switch to your production project

Point your product at your production project, and stop sending test data to your development project. [Managing Projects](/docs/orgs-and-projects/managing-projects) · [Developer Environments](/docs/tracking-best-practices/developer-environments)

### Deploy your tracking

Review the code, then deploy it through your usual release process.

### Compare what arrives to what you tested

Check that your production events match what you saw in development. [Debugging](/docs/tracking-best-practices/debugging) · [Lexicon](/docs/data-governance/lexicon)

No events showing? Start with the [Sending Data FAQ](/troubleshooting/sending-data).

On an Enterprise plan? Approve your new events so your team can see them. [Event Approval](/docs/data-governance/event-approval)

### Watch for problems

Set up alerts so you hear about drops or spikes before your team does. [Alerts](/docs/features/alerts)

On an Enterprise plan? [Data Volume Monitoring](/docs/data-governance/data-volume-monitoring) watches event volume for you.

## Next step

When your events reach production and match what you tested, go to [4. Roll out to your team](/guides/get-started/roll-out-to-your-team).
