Data only helps when people use it. This step puts your first report in front of your teammates and gets them comfortable asking their own questions.

**Done when:**

- The right teammates have access
- They know how to use Mixpanel
- They can answer their own questions

<Note>
  You do this step whether you set up with AI or yourself.
</Note>

## Same step, with or without AI

### Build your first report

Pick the question that matters most and build a report that answers it. [Reports](/docs/reports) · [Core Reports](/guides/guides-by-topic/core-reports)

### Save it to a board

Put your first report on a board so your team has somewhere to start. [Boards](/docs/boards) · [Create Boards](/guides/guides-by-topic/core-reports/create-boards)

### Write your business context

Using the Mixpanel Agent or MCP? Add your business context to your production project so your team gets accurate answers. [Business Context](/docs/business-context) · [Write business context](/guides/guides-by-workflow/write-business-context)

### Give teammates access

Add the teammates who will use Mixpanel. [Give your team access](/set-up-mixpanel/implement-and-test#give-your-team-access)

### Help teammates learn the basics

Point new users to an overview they can read or watch. [Mixpanel Introduction](/guides/mixpanel-introduction)

### Decide who owns your data

Pick someone to keep your events and definitions in order as your team grows. [Govern Your Mixpanel Data](/guides/guides-by-topic/govern-data)
